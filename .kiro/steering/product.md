# Product steering: Smriti, The Revision Graveyard
- Users: self-study exam aspirants (UPSC, IFoS (Indian Forest Service), state exams) revising on a phone.
- Promise: the app decides what to revise tonight; forgotten topics are shown as ghosts.
- Tone: spooky but readable. Every spooky element must carry meaning (ghost = due, grave at rest = scheduled).
- Never require sign-up. Data stays on the device.
